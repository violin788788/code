Directly through the people.
Presentation and proposal from the Zurich section to the Congress of the International Workers' Association to be opened in Basel on September 6, 1869.
The experience of the last few decades has thoroughly cured the working class of Europe of the delusion that imperial democracy, imperial socialism, i.e. h. the dictatorship of an individual is able or even willing to do something for the social upliftment of the working masses. Nothing but fake reforms, sand in the eyes, in reality the worker is more than ever a tax machine and cannon fodder.
Since Bonaparte's coup, the belief has been spread from above among the working masses with great cunning that the political or state form has nothing to do with social, i.e. social, reforms; Therefore, workers should not concern themselves with politics but only with their social situation.
